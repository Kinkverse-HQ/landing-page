# Notice

This package is an independent USL implementation based on the user's supplied workflow and a review of publicly described ideas associated with Barbara Minto. It does not copy Barbara Minto's textbook, course materials, exercises, or proprietary examples, and it is not affiliated with or endorsed by Minto Books International, McKinsey & Company, or Pearson.

Research reviewed on 2026-09-01:

- Barbara Minto, [The Minto Pyramid Principle concept](https://www.barbaraminto.com/concept) and [textbook overview](https://www.barbaraminto.com/textbook).
- Pearson, [The Pyramid Principle, 3rd edition](https://www.pearson.com/en-gb/subject-catalog/p/the-pyramid-principle/P200000015259/9781292763255), ISBN 9781292763255, published 2026.
- McKinsey & Company, [Barbara Minto: “MECE: I invented it, so I get to say how to pronounce it”](https://www.mckinsey.com/alumni/news-and-events/global-news/alumni-news/barbara-minto-mece-i-invented-it-so-i-get-to-say-how-to-pronounce-it).
- Bogaerds-Hazenberg et al., [A Meta-Analysis on the Effects of Text Structure Instruction on Reading Comprehension in the Upper Elementary Grades](https://doi.org/10.1002/rrq.311), *Reading Research Quarterly* 56(3), 2021.
- Rickards et al., [Prose organization and recall](https://doi.org/10.1016/0361-476X(76)90053-9), *Contemporary Educational Psychology* 1(4), 1976.

The empirical studies support the narrower proposition that explicit and salient text structure can aid comprehension, summarization, or recall in the populations studied. They do not independently validate the full Minto method for executive or professional communication.
