## 3. What counts as a day

An ordinary qualifying day requires the day’s check-in within the displayed response window, compliant evidence where requested, and **three assigned peer reviews** where available. Evidence and review requirements are shown before submission. Approval depends on the published challenge criteria, not the reviewer’s attraction to you, payment or personal relationship.

If too few eligible reviews are available, we provide a reasonable alternative or an administrative resolution; our lack of reviewers must not automatically destroy your progress. A platform delivery or review fault can be corrected after examination of the records. A disputed check-in can be appealed through the in-game contact or our support address.

A completion badge is awarded after **31 qualifying days**, including properly applied freeze days. It means that your account completed the platform’s rules, using check-ins, peer assessments and allowed freezes. It **does not certify 31 continuously observed days of physical restraint**, identity, health, honesty in every circumstance or consent to any future interaction.

## 4. Freezes and interrupted progression

No freeze is given at entry: **two freezes** are granted on the **first day of the challenge**, with the day-1 lesson. You may hold at most **three** at once and obtain at most **five during this season**, counting the first-day freezes, earned freezes and bought freezes. A freeze can also be bought for **600 Stars**, only for a day still to come; it counts toward both limits. Any opportunity to earn further freezes states its conditions before participation; extra freezes are not guaranteed simply because the seasonal maximum is five.

An available freeze may protect a missed eligible day under the displayed rule. A protected day counts toward the 31-day total, but does **not** itself generate a check-in reward, review reward, mission reward or leaderboard score.

A missed day without a usable freeze interrupts the completion run. A restart can then be bought for **900 Stars**: one per season, only after an interruption, provided you have at least ten qualifying days and the season has not ended. It reactivates your run from the next scheduled check; it does not reset your counter, make up any missed day or grant any freeze. No restart is automatic, and there is no guarantee that a late restart can be completed. We can correct errors or make a reasonable safety accommodation, but we do not sell a false statement that an uncompleted challenge was completed.

Declining or not completing an optional mission does not by itself invalidate an ordinary qualifying day. Sensitive-profile personalization is optional and subject to its specific consent. ## 7. Optional Stars and conversion into Treats

Stars are optional internal game credits governed by the [Stars and Treats Terms](stars-and-treats-terms.md). They are not Telegram Stars, money held on deposit or a payment for the completion badge.

For this season, **six Stars validly allocated produce one Treat** (6 Stars = 1 Treat). Only whole Treats are produced; leftover Stars stay in your balance. Conversions take place when your missions are approved and as your days are approved; timing, the speed of your check-ins and other disclosed criteria can change the pace and, for the conversion tied to daily progress, move the result moderately away from this base rate. The conversion information explains what can vary.

Stars already converted are not counted twice; refunded or reversed Stars and Stars gifted away are excluded from your allocation. Stars that have not been converted are not silently consumed. Gifts received and validly allocated belong to the recipient’s run, not simultaneously to the sender’s.

**This is a reward conversion, not repayment of your purchase price.** Treats have no fixed euro value. They can be used as eligible product discounts in the Club’s Boutique under the terms offered there when an order is placed. Existing credited Treats are available for eligible redemption without waiting for the season to end, subject to verification of a genuinely disputed transaction.

Stopping early does not earn the completion badge. It does not automatically confiscate Treats already validly earned or unused purchased Stars. The economy terms explain continuing balances, refunds and programme closure. No reward exists unless the applicable feature and its terms are actually offered; once you make an eligible allocation under this published conversion promise, we cannot later call that promise merely discretionary.

## 8. Leaderboard and visibility

The leaderboard is **opt-in**. Participation in the challenge is not permission to list you publicly: you appear only if you have chosen to make your profile visible, in the bot or in the Kinkverse app. The opt-in explains the pseudonym, score and other fields displayed. You may leave the public leaderboard without losing otherwise earned completion progress. A completion badge is also hidden unless you choose to display it.

Score and spendable balance are separate. The score is 100 points per approved day, plus 1 point per Treat obtained through a mission. Stars earn points only when converted by a mission: buying Stars can therefore influence the score indirectly, as can the subscription required to take part. Days covered by a freeze, gifts and completion conversions earn no points. We do not imply that a ranking is purely skill-based if purchases affect it. Ties, review errors and collusion are resolved using disclosed rules and reviewable records, not an undisclosed change after the outcome.

## 9. Products, partners and separate promotions

Apart from the prize weeks described below, there is no guaranteed physical prize, partner, weekly drop, social-media prize, catalogue item, collection-opening day or discount on a named product under these rules. Boutique access and stock can vary. A Treat balance is not a reservation of merchandise and cannot pay delivery charges.

### Prize weeks

- **Windows.** Each prize week runs from Sunday 00:00 to the following Sunday 00:00, Paris time. The first counts 4 to 11 October 2026, then 11 to 18 October, 18 to 25 October and 25 October to 1 November: four weeks, up to the close of registration. Points from 1 to 3 October count toward the season score, not toward the first prize week.
- **Eligibility.** Your run must be in progress (neither completed nor withdrawn), your profile must be visible on the leaderboard, your score in the window must be above zero, and you must not already have won a prize week this season: a member can win only once.
- **Score counted.** The score of section 8, limited to the window: 100 points per approved day and 1 point per Treat obtained through a mission during that week.
- **Selection.** The highest eligible score of the week is proposed to the operator, who confirms and marks the winner after the week has ended, with a recorded reason. The prize itself is allocated by hand, outside the game.
- **Ties.** Equal scores share the same rank. No further tie-break rule is set at present: in a tie for first place, the operator chooses the winner.
- **Announcement.** The winner receives a message in the bot. A message is also sent to users who have started the bot, naming the winner if their profile is visible, otherwise referring to a member who keeps their profile private.
- **Prize.** A brand-new KINK3D cage of the winner’s choice, according to the game guide. The prize is shipped to the address the winner provides. No cash alternative is promised.

Any other leaderboard promotion, repost campaign or partner gift will have **separate rules before entry**, identifying eligibility, dates, territories, selection, tie-breaking, prize and fulfillment. A discussion, teaser or possible partnership does not establish that a promotion has opened. Once a promotion is actually offered, its specific commitments cannot be cancelled merely by relying on this paragraph.
