Timing and distributions can vary as disclosed in the game rules. Treats already credited can be used for eligible Boutique offers without waiting for the season to finish. A conversion, an optional mission reward, a promotional grant and leaderboard score are separate events. No unit can be counted twice merely because more than one application displays it.

## 7. How Treats work in the Club’s Boutique

Treats are used **only as a product-specific loyalty discount** or on other eligible offers shown before use, not as money paid to a seller, delivery provider or another member. For each eligible product or variant, the product page or order summary shows the number of Treats required for the offered full product discount, any lower cap, conditions and eligible quantity. Products that can only be obtained through a specific promotional offer are identified as such.

Where partial redemption is offered, using part of the displayed full-discount threshold gives the corresponding proportional product discount, subject to disclosed currency rounding and caps. For example, where a product shows 1,000 Treats for a full product discount, 250 eligible Treats can cover 25% of that product price if partial redemption is offered. This is an illustration of the calculation, not a promised catalogue offer or euro value of a Treat.

**Delivery, shipping charges, customs duties and other expressly excluded charges remain payable by the customer in money.** Treats cannot pay for delivery. The final order shows the product price, Treats used, discount and cash total before confirmation. A 100% product discount does not mean free delivery.

The available catalogue, stocks, product prices, thresholds, territorial delivery and promotional conditions at the time of ordering determine what can be redeemed. We do not guarantee a particular item, partner, product line or constant euro conversion rate. Nevertheless, we honour accepted orders, individually awarded offers and protected accrued rights; this clause is not permission to empty the entire programme of useful redemption or retrospectively reprice an accepted order.

An order is subject to the Boutique’s sale and fulfillment terms. Statutory conformity, product safety and applicable withdrawal rights are not removed merely because Treats supplied all or part of the product discount.
